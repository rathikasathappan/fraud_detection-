    A few useful details

    Questions, answered.

    What does cold-start mean here?

    A merchant is cold-start when there is little or no transaction history available. The assessment can still consider onboarding attributes and relationships among shared entities.

    How can a graph help assess a new merchant?

    Relationships such as a shared device or account can provide context that a standalone profile does not show. The graph view helps an investigator inspect those connections.

    Can I understand why a profile was flagged?

    Yes. The assessment presents contributing reasons so that an operator can review the signals and make a policy-led decision.

    What happens in the interactive demo?

    It scores one of three fixed synthetic examples. The request is predict-only, rate limited, requires no login, and does not save assessment inputs or results.

    Are the metrics based on live merchants?

    No. Metrics on this page come from simulated sample data and saved model evaluation results. They are not live production performance claims.
